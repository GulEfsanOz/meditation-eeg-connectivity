# EEG Dataset

The EEG dataset used in this research is available through
the L-FAME Dataset Benchmark on Hugging Face.

**Dataset:** L-FAME (Longitudinal Meditation EEG Dataset)

**Access the dataset:**
https://huggingface.co/datasets/L-FAME-Dataset-Benchmark/L-FAME

## Study Data

The connectivity analyses presented at the Society for
Neuroscience 2026 conference used a subset of 31 participants:

- HK meditation group: 16 participants
- SA meditation group: 15 participants

The analyses included resting-state and meditation EEG
recordings collected before and after meditation training.

## Data Availability

The original EEG recordings are hosted on Hugging Face
and are not duplicated in this GitHub repository.

Please refer to the dataset's Hugging Face page for
access instructions, documentation, and applicable terms.
